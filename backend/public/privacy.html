Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 • Version 1.0

Summary: AI Outfit processes your uploaded photos strictly for generating virtual clothing fitting simulations. We never sell your personal data, never create facial recognition profiles, and automatically delete temporary images after processing.

1. Introduction

Welcome to AI Outfit: Try On Clothes ("the App", "we", "our", or "us"), provided by Difmo Pvt Ltd. We respect your privacy and are deeply committed to protecting the personal data and photos you provide while using our virtual try-on services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is processed, and your rights regarding your data when you use the AI Outfit mobile application and related web services.

2. Information We Collect

To provide high-quality virtual try-on styling, we collect:

  • User-Uploaded Photos: Full-body, upper-body, or clothing photos that you voluntarily select from your device gallery or capture with your camera.
  • Selected Garments & Styling Preferences: Outfits, clothing templates, categories, and focus areas you select.
  • Technical & Device Data: Non-personally identifiable technical diagnostic logs, operating system version, and general error telemetry to ensure reliable app operation.

3. How We Use and Process Photos (AI Virtual Try-On)

Photos uploaded to AI Outfit are processed through neural diffusion models (IDM-VTON) solely to synthesize virtual clothing transformations onto the body in the photograph.

  • No Facial Recognition or Biometrics: We do NOT extract facial geometric data, biometric templates, or identity profiles. Contours are used strictly for inpainting boundaries during clothing synthesis.
  • No AI Model Training on Private Data: Your uploaded photos are never used to train public machine learning or foundational AI models.
  • Confidentiality: Your photos are never published, shared with third-party advertisers, or made public without your explicit consent.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

We believe in strict data minimization:

  • Transient Processing: Photos uploaded for virtual try-on are cached in a temporary secure directory solely for the duration required to generate the styled output.
  • Saved Wardrobe Looks: Any looks you choose to save remain on your local device storage or temporary session cache.
  • Data Deletion Requests: You may request immediate deletion of any residual server logs or uploaded files associated with your session by emailing our Data Protection Officer at dineshkumartlbupi@gmail.com. Requests are fulfilled within 48 hours.

5. Security of Your Information

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security controls to protect your data:

  • All network communications between the mobile application and our servers are encrypted using HTTPS with TLS 1.3.
  • Backend servers are hosted on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure (Render) protected by active firewalls and strict access restrictions.

6. Third-Party Services

To deliver virtual try-on transformations, our application coordinates with cloud inference providers:

  • Render Cloud: Hosts our secure application backend and API services.
  • Neural Inference Pipelines: Executes latent diffusion virtual try-on algorithms on isolated, secure inference nodes.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not targeted at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal photos, please contact us immediately for immediate removal.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". Continued use of the application after changes constitute acceptance of the revised policy.
