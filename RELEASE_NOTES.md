### 🌟 Key Highlights & What's New

1. **Neural Virtual Try-On Engine**
   - End-to-end photorealistic clothing simulation with accurate body warping, realistic fabric wrinkles, and ambient lighting blending.
   - Robust fallback and multi-tier routing for uninterrupted service.

2. **Luxury Wardrobe Catalog**
   - Curated collection spanning **Cozy Dresses**, **Work Shirts & Blazers**, **Party Wear & Evening Gowns**, **Royal Indian Ethnic (Sarees & Anarkalis)**, and **Men's Fashion**.
   - Filter by categories, tags, and occasions.

3. **Smart Targeted Body Framing**
   - Select focus areas: **Upper Body** (shirts, jackets), **Lower Body** (pants, skirts), or **Full Body** (gowns, dresses, suits).
   - Guidance overlay ensures optimal photo positioning.

4. **Interactive Hardware-Accelerated Split Slider**
   - 60fps fluid draggable split comparison screen.
   - Immediate visual comparison between original photo and new styled look.

5. **HD Export & Social Sharing**
   - Save high-resolution transformed photos directly to phone gallery.
   - One-tap sharing to Instagram, WhatsApp, Facebook, and more.

6. **Privacy & Security**
   - User photos are encrypted in transit and processed securely.
   - Strict privacy policy and terms of service compliant with Google Play policies.
